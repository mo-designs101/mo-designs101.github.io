Privacy Policy

Schema Scout, Tag Weight and Launch Check (Chrome extensions)
Effective date: 16 November 2026

This policy covers three Chrome extensions published by Mo Designs ("we", "us"): Schema Scout, Tag Weight and Launch Check. In short: the extensions do their work inside your browser, we do not collect or receive any information about you or the pages you visit, and we run no analytics or tracking.

What the extensions access

An extension only runs when you click its toolbar icon. At that moment it reads the page in your current tab to produce its report:

  • Schema Scout reads the page's structured data (JSON-LD, microdata) and related page details such as the title, headings and meta tags.
  • Tag Weight reads the browser's performance records for the page: the addresses of the files the page loaded, their sizes and timings.
  • Launch Check reads the page's meta tags, headings, images, links and form fields.

This information is processed on your device and shown to you in the extension's popup. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not kept after you close the popup. Reports are saved only if you choose to export one, and then only as a file on your own device.

Information stored on your device

If you activate a premium license, the extension stores your license key, an activation identifier and the date it was last checked, using Chrome's local extension storage. This stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by choosing "Remove license" in the popup or by uninstalling the extension.

Purchases

Premium licenses are sold through Polar (Polar Software, Inc.), which acts as merchant of record. When you buy, Polar collects the information needed to complete the sale, such as your name, email address, billing country and payment details, under its own privacy policy at polar.sh/legal/privacy. We never receive your card details. Polar shares order information with us (such as your name, email address and what you bought), which we use only to provide the license, give support, handle refunds and meet legal and accounting obligations.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: to read the current page when you click the extension icon.
  • storage: to remember your license on your device.
  • Access to api.polar.sh: to verify your license.
  • Optional access to other websites (Launch Check only): requested only when you start a link check, so the extension can test whether each link works. You can decline, and you can revoke it later in Chrome's extension settings.

How we use and share information

We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, do not use it for purposes unrelated to the extensions' single purpose, and do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The extensions are tools for web professionals and are not directed at children.
