How premium works

Each extension is free to install and use. Premium is a one-time purchase per extension. After checkout you receive a license key; paste it into the extension to unlock the premium features. One key works on up to three browsers, and it does not expire. Payments are handled by Polar, which acts as merchant of record.

Private by design

The extensions run only when you click them and do their work inside your browser. They collect no data and contain no analytics. Details are in the privacy policy.
